Introduction to
Bayesian Modeling

A two-day, hands-on workshop for early-career researchers — from Bayes' theorem to hierarchical models, mostly by doing.

  • Two days
  • Taught in English
  • JASP & R
  • No prior Bayesian experience needed
Setup guide — start here Download all materials GitHub repository

Two things to do before day 1

1. Install the software. JASP for both days, plus R, RStudio and brms for day 2. The setup guide walks you through it and includes a script that checks whether everything works. Please do this in advance — we cannot troubleshoot installations during the sessions.

2. Bring a dataset of your own, ideally one you have already analysed with frequentist methods. On day 2 you will re-analyse it the Bayesian way. What to bring →
